Planetary position relative to the star. Determines if liquid water can exist on the surface.
SURFACE
Terrain stability and composition. Assesses suitability for landing and base construction.
HYDROSPHERE
Presence of water in all forms: oceans, rivers, ice caps, and subsurface reserves.
ATMOSPHERE
Atmospheric density and composition. Critical for pressure regulation and radiation shielding.
BREATHABILITY
Oxygen content and air toxicity. Determines if humans can breathe without life support systems.
CLIMATE
Temperature stability and weather patterns. Evaluates long-term habitability conditions.
GRAVITY
Surface gravitational pull. Affects human health, construction, and equipment operation.
MAGNETIC
Magnetosphere strength. Protects the surface from solar wind and cosmic radiation.

Frequency and intensity of stellar eruptions. High levels pose radiation hazards to colonists.
